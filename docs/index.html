DraftPilot for Skool — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-09

DraftPilot for Skool ("the extension") is a browser extension that drafts reply text for posts and comments on skool.com.

What the extension stores

  • Your Anthropic API key, your tone preference, a daily draft counter and, if you have Pro, your Gumroad license key. All of this is stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local). It is never sent to us.

What the extension sends, and to whom

  • When you click "Draft reply", the text of the post or comment you are replying to (and up to five recent comments in that thread) is sent to Anthropic, together with your API key, to generate the draft. That transfer is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • If you enter a Pro license key, the key is sent to Gumroad's license verification service to confirm it is valid.
  • The extension has no server of its own. It sends nothing anywhere else.

What we collect

  • Nothing. We do not collect analytics, usage data, personal information, or your content.

What we do not do

  • We do not post on your behalf. We do not sell or share data. We do not use data for advertising.

Your control

  • Remove your key or uninstall the extension at any time. Stored data is deleted with it.
